# DEC-0003: JCR top-quartile submission readiness

## Owner instruction

The owner set an active journal goal and selected Journal Citation Reports /
Web of Science as the ranking system. The concrete project objective is an
evidence-bound manuscript and package ready for submission to an eligible
top-quartile journal. Acceptance remains an external editorial outcome and is
not a promised completion condition.

Use the Journal Impact Factor quartile, recorded with the metric year, subject
category, journal identity and source. Do not substitute SCImago quartiles,
CiteScore percentiles or Journal Citation Indicator quartiles. Clarivate
assigns a JIF rank and quartile separately for each subject category; a journal
can consequently have different category ranks. See the
[official ranking explanation](https://clarivate.com/academia-government/blog/a-primer-on-ties-in-the-jcr/).

## Effect on venue choice

The prior JIIS → KAIS → IJMLC preference in [DEC-0002](0002-journal-target-order.md)
remains a record of the owner's preferred venues. Eligibility under the new
goal must be verified before activating a submission target. None of those
venues is currently verified as meeting the requested ranking threshold.
An earlier preferred venue cannot satisfy the new goal merely by retaining its
place in the list.

Publisher home pages currently expose JIF values but not category quartiles.
Preliminary secondary listings indicate that the preferred sequence may not
meet the threshold; these listings are screening signals, not primary-source
verification. The direct JCR profile was not retrievable in the current browser
session. Preserve that verification gap explicitly in the
[venue register](../manuscript/journal-targets.toml).

Screen Knowledge-Based Systems, Information Sciences and Expert Systems with
Applications as additional candidates. University-maintained records suggest
they merit checking, but category-specific official verification and scope
review remain open. This is a research shortlist, not a new submission order.
Recheck the applicable ranking before actual submission and distinguish the
metric year from the release/access year.

## Scientific completion conditions

- Reconcile the current sampling/gradient diagnostics, retaining adverse results.
- Register the final research question and full-study protocol before new test
  inspection; use training/validation for model and checkpoint decisions.
- Close runtime reproducibility, original-comparator parity, appropriate
  uncertainty reporting and independent-source replication.
- Admit a new immutable evidence release and derive manuscript claims from wiki.
- Resolve the eligible venue, current formatting, author declarations and
  evidence-bound submission snapshot.

Journal quartile does not replace novelty, technical correctness, reproducible
evaluation or scope fit. The [current article backlog](../manuscript/JIIS-Journal.md)
remains useful scientific preparation while venue screening proceeds.
`wiki/` stays canonical and all solving/heavy execution remains Slurm-only.
The existing frozen release and `paper/CURRENT` remain unchanged.
