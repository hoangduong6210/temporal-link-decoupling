Upload DYU_Overleaf.zip as a new Overleaf project. Select XeLaTeX and main.tex. The complete bibliography and diagram are embedded in main.tex. No external images, bibliography or research dataset is needed to compile.

The delivered PDF uses Times New Roman / Songti TC. Overleaf uses the included TeX-distribution font fallback (Termes / Fandol) if those fonts are unavailable, so page breaks may differ. Do not substitute an unverified font file.

prepare.py and build.sh regenerate verified numerical inputs when run within the complete research repository. They are not required for the standalone Overleaf compile. export_word.py additionally requires the repository Word/template.docx, Pandoc and python-docx.
