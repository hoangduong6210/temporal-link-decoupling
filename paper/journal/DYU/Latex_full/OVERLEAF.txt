Upload DYU_Overleaf.zip as a new Overleaf project. Select XeLaTeX and main.tex. The bibliography and vector diagram are embedded; compilation needs no research data or external images.

The delivered PDF uses Times New Roman and MingLiU. The source uses installed fonts when available and otherwise falls back to TeX-distribution fonts (Termes/Fandol). Overleaf page breaks may differ. No proprietary font is bundled.

prepare.py and build.sh verify and regenerate numerical inputs in the complete research repository; these checks require NumPy and the corpus files. They are not needed for standalone compilation. Word export additionally requires ../Word/template.docx, Pandoc, python-docx and Pillow.
